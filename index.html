What the bench checks

    

      
CHECK 1
Line items add to the subtotal
Every line amount in the document is added up and compared with the subtotal the document states.

      
CHECK 2
Subtotal plus tax and shipping equals the total
The stated subtotal, tax, and shipping are added and compared with the stated total.

      
CHECK 3
Each line: quantity x unit price equals its amount
Every line is multiplied out on its own, so one wrong line is named on its own.

      
CHECK 4
Tax amount matches the stated rate x subtotal
Where the document states a tax rate, the tax amount is checked against that rate.

What it cannot do

    

      
Input
Pasted text and plain text files (.txt and .csv) only. Photos, scans, handwriting, and PDFs are not read.

      
Processing
Runs in your browser. No model is called and nothing is uploaded, so the document never leaves this device.

      
Parser
Heuristic. Patterns and rules look for labelled fields and amount columns. Fields without labels may be missed, and unusual layouts can be misread. The bench shows its working so you can spot that.

      
Formats
Best effort on unfamiliar formats. Documents that mix number conventions, such as commas used both as thousands separators and as decimal points, can be parsed wrongly.

      
Tax
One tax line. A document with several different tax lines is not fully modelled.

      
Output
The extracted fields, the four checks with their figures, and the raw JSON. A check that cannot be computed is marked skipped, with the reason.

    

  


  

    
Questions

    

      What can I run through it?
      
Invoice or receipt text that you paste in, or a plain text file (.txt or .csv). The full list of what is not read is in the limits above.

    

    

      What does a failed check mean?
      
It means the figures in the document do not agree with each other, and the bench shows both figures and the exact difference. It does not fix the document and it does not say which figure is the right one. That judgement stays with you.

    

    

      What happens when a field is missing?
      
The bench writes "Not found in this text" for that field. It never guesses a value, and a check that cannot be computed is marked as skipped, with the reason.

    

    

      Does it cost anything?
      
No. The bench is free to use in your browser. There are no pricing tiers, no trial, and no subscription attached to it.

    

    

      Why sign in at all?
      
It opens the demo workspace: the same bench plus a run history kept on this device. The sign in page states the demo terms before you continue.
