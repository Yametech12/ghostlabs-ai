What it reads and where it fails

      
The honest list. If a document shape is not covered here, expect "Not found" fields and skipped checks rather than a guessed answer.

    

    

      

        
Supported inputs

        
Pasted invoice or receipt text, and .txt or .csv files opened from your device (read locally in the browser, never uploaded). The sample inputs above run through the same extraction engine as your own text.

        
Not supported: images, scans, PDFs, photos, and handwriting. Copy the text out of a photo first (Live Text on iOS, Google Lens on Android), then paste it.

      

      

        
Numbers, dates, tolerance

        
Handles decimal points and decimal commas, thousands separators, parentheses for negative amounts, and the currency symbols and codes it can recognise ($, EUR, GBP, PHP, JPY signs and codes). Ambiguous dates such as 01/02/2026 are read as day, month, year.

        
Tolerance rule: a check passes when its computed figure is within 0.02 of the figure printed on the document. Amounts are compared after rounding to two decimal places, so a one cent rounding difference in the source document does not fail a check.

      

      

        
Tests and known limits

        
The extraction engine is covered by 129 automated battery expectations: 46 engine unit tests, 62 expectations across 6 adversarial documents, and 21 expectations across 2 sample documents. This build passes 129 of 129.

        
Still limited: split tax rates are only checked when each tax line states its own rate and the base it applies to; a tax line without a stated base is checked against the subtotal and the result says so. When no subtotal is printed, tax and total are checked against the line items sum plus shipping, and the check says it used that implied base. Line items need a trailing amount per row; rows in other shapes can be missed. The vendor is a best-effort guess from the first header lines.
